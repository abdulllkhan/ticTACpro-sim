    How to play

    Line of one size

    Three of your pieces of the same size in a row, column or diagonal wins.

    Bullseye

    Stack all three sizes of your color in one cell — instant win.

    Slots are independent

    Each cell has a Small, a Medium and a Large slot. Pieces never cover each other — a cell can hold your Small and their Large.

    Nine pieces each

    3 Small, 3 Medium, 3 Large per player. Red moves first. If all 18 pieces land with no win, it's a draw.

    Fun fact: this game is solved — with perfect play, Red (moving first) always wins. Expert difficulty knows the winning lines. Can you find them?
